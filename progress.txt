HOW TO EDIT CONTENT
-------------------
Any subpage is regenerated from scripts/bodies/<slug>.html:

    node scripts/wrap-page.mjs privacy       # rebuild just privacy
    node scripts/wrap-page.mjs all           # rebuild every body file found

The head <title>, <meta description> and all body copy can be edited in the body file
and regenerated in one command. Header and footer come from scripts/partials/* and are
shared across every page.

The homepage (index.html) is NOT generated — it stays hand-tuned because of its sky
animation and one-off live-brief wiring.
