### 02.1 Who this is for

| # | Sector | Examples |

| 4 | **Health & aesthetics** | Dental, dermatology, aesthetic clinics, vets, physiotherapy |
| 5 | **Boutique retail** | Bookshops, concept stores, specialty grocery, ateliers |

**Shared traits across sectors (non-negotiable filter):**
- One to three physical locations (or one with plans to grow).
- Reputation-driven: 60 %+ of customers come from word-of-mouth, Google reviews, or Instagram.
- Owner-operator or owner-led: the founder answers WhatsApp.
- Annual revenue typically **S/ 400k – S/ 4M** (USD ~110k–1.1M).
- Bilingual potential (tourists, expats, or Peruvian bilingual audience).

### 02.2 Who this is not for (route to Rainey Laguna Studios or decline)

- **Big chains.** More than ~6 locations. Route to Studios or decline.
- **B2B SaaS / tech startups.** Not our model. Decline.
- **Pure e-commerce** (no physical room, no neighbourhood story). Decline.
- **Other agencies** looking for white-label. Decline.
- **Real-estate development** and other "scale-by-capital" businesses. Decline.
- **Professional services with no physical room** (pure remote coaching, etc.). Usually decline; case-by-case.
- **Non-hospitality design services across categories** → route to Rainey Laguna Studios.

### 02.3 Ideal-client signals (move fast on these)

- Owner replies to WhatsApp within an hour.
- Already has 50 + Google reviews with a 4.4 + average.
- Instagram exists and has been posted to within the last 14 days.
- A competitor just redesigned their site and is now eating the client's Google traffic.
- The owner mentions a specific complaint about their current web vendor.

### 02.4 Anti-signals (walk, slowly and politely)

- Wants "just a template site for S/ 500."
- Wants to pay in kind ("free meals for a year").
- Three decision-makers with no designated primary.
- Never replies the same day and expects us to.

## 04 · Signature mechanics on `raineylaguna.com`

These are the things the site _does_ that no other Peruvian web-agency site does. They are not marketing copy — they are running software.

### 04.1 Live Brief _(shipping v0.9)_
A rotating, sector-tagged competitive brief that regenerates on the homepage every few hours, telling the visitor what their sector's competitors did this week in Lima. Five sectors rotate. Teaches Vigía in public.

### 04.2 Bilingual time-of-day moods _(shipping)_
Nine tonal variants across the day, each WCAG AA-contrast-locked. The site reads differently at 7 am vs 10 pm — colour, type weight, and imagery shift.

### 04.3 JSON-LD-first SEO _(shipping)_
Every page ships an OfferCatalog + FAQPage + BlogPosting graph. Optimised for AI search (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google AI Overview) as much as for classical Google.

### 04.4 Plausible analytics + Formspree fallback _(shipping)_
Privacy-first analytics, guarded by hostname. Forms POST to Vigía's backend when online, Formspree when Vigía is down. Zero tracking cookies.

### 04.5 The Live Audit _(Sprint 2)_
Visitor types their URL, gets a 90-second competitive audit live on the page. See §03.2. First of its kind in Perú.

### 04.6 Garúa AI _(Sprint 2, blocked on Meta)_
Weather-triggered marketing: humidity-spike → three drafted IG posts + WA broadcast + site-hero copy → owner one-taps approval → published within minutes. See `docs/meta-whatsapp-instagram-submission.md` in the `raineylagunastudios` repo for the submission copy.

### 04.7 Care Vivo / Self-Optimising Site _(Sprint 3)_
Claude runs a weekly A/B experiment per client, ships the winner. Uses Thompson sampling for low-traffic cafés.

### 04.8 Socio / AI Co-Founder _(Sprint 3)_
Per-client Claude trained on brand voice + menu + analytics + two-year email/WA archive. Answers in the owner's voice via WhatsApp.

### 04.9 Source-Code Commons _(policy live 2 May 2026)_
Every client site goes public on `github.com/raineylaguna-commons` from day one with the client's consent. Forces us to compete on strategy, not code. Policy lives in the Studios repo at `COMMONS.md`.

### 04.10 Live Audit · email capture + Cal.com hand-off _(shipped 3 May 2026)_
The Live Audit (§04.5 / §03.2) now ends with three exits: (a) WhatsApp Stuart with the warns count, (b) book a 30-minute call via Cal.com — slug read from `<meta name="rl-cal-booking">`, swappable without a deploy, (c) email-the-report capture form posting to Vigía `/api/audit-email-request`. A silent telemetry ping fires to `/api/audit-completed` even when the visitor takes none of these, so the studio knows an audit ran. Backend contract documented at the bottom of `scripts/live-audit.js`.

### 04.11 Care templates · public download _(shipped 3 May 2026)_
`/servicios/care/` now offers three real templates we use every month with Care clients — content calendar (CSV), maintenance checklist (Markdown), review-response scripts (Markdown · ES + EN). Files live at `/templates/care/`. No email-gate: the page reads "sin formulario, sin email." The templates double as proof-of-process for prospective clients.

## 05 · Voice and tone

### 05.1 Voice in one paragraph

> Rainey Laguna writes like a senior tradesman explaining the job. Plain, short, specific, warm but not cute. Never pushy. Never hypey. Humour is dry and deadpan. Sincere. Honest about limits. When we don't know something, we say so. We use "nosotros" (or "we") even when it is just Stuart, because the work is plural and the support is plural. Spanish primary, English second.

### 05.2 Rules (non-negotiable)

- **Spanish primary, English second.** `es-PE` is the default `lang` attribute. English is the mirror, never the source.
- **Never call a client a "client" in public copy.** Use their name or "el lugar" / "the place."
- **Never use "leverage," "synergies," "best-in-class," "world-class," "cutting-edge," "revolutionary."** Ban list.
- **Never say "AI."** Say "asistente," "Claude," "el agente," or specifically what the AI does. AI is a tool, not a sales pitch.
- **Numbers are specific.** "9 de cada 10" beats "la mayoría."
- **Prices are public.** Anchor prices appear in copy and in proposals. Negotiation is transparent.
- **No emojis.** Ever. On the site, in emails, in proposals. The only exception: a single "·" as a separator.
- **Accents are correct.** "Garúa" always has an acute accent. Pet peeve.

### 05.3 Words we like

> garúa · taller · oficio · mano · archivo · seña · ficha · colofón · pie de imprenta · bitácora · paciencia · vecindario · barrio · reputación · respuesta · observación · clima · café · menú · mesa · paso · próxima

### 05.4 Tone shifts

- **Homepage:** confident, specific, slightly aphoristic.
- **Case studies:** forensic. Numbers, dates, weeks, outcomes.
- **Emails:** warmer. First-name basis. Humour ok.
- **Proposals:** auditor-meets-carpenter. Every line justified.
- **Error states:** apologetic, practical. Never blame the user.

| Rule | `rgba(20, 36, 46, 0.18)` | Hairlines |

The palette is warm, paper-toned, gold-accented. It reads "a good book" not "an app."

### 06.2 Typography

- **Display + body:** `Fraunces` (variable: `wght` 200–900, `opsz` 9–144, `slnt` 0– -10)
- **UI + mono:** `JetBrains Mono` (wght 300–500)
- Rule: Fraunces italic is never used outside display. Mono is never used for body.

### 06.3 Grid + spacing

- 12-column grid, 24 px gutters, max width 1280 px.
- Vertical rhythm at 8 px base, 24 px paragraph gap.
- Hairlines at 0.5 px where possible (1 px fallback).

### 06.4 Imagery

- Real photography of real places. Never stock.
- Shot in ambient light (garúa-diffused is ideal).
- Never more than one person's face per image (crops / fragments preferred).

### 06.5 Relationship to Studios

Studios is the **inverted mirror**: graphite + ivory + vermilion. Same Fraunces + Mono. Family resemblance, opposite palette. If you confuse one with the other visually, we have failed.

## 07 · Architecture

### 07.1 Domains
- `raineylaguna.com` — canonical
- `raineylaguna.com/en/` — English mirror
- `www.raineylaguna.com` — 301 to apex
- `raineylagunastudios.com` — parent brand (separate site, separate repo)

### 07.2 Stack
- **Front-end:** static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework. Target deploy: **Cloudflare Pages** (free, global edge). Build via custom Node scripts in `scripts/`.
- **Back-end (Vigía):** Next.js at `c:\Users\Stu\vigia`, deploy to **Vercel Hobby**, custom domain `vigia.raineylaguna.com`. API: `/api/brief`, `/api/audit`, `/api/audit-completed`, `/api/audit-email-request`.
- **Database:** **Neon Postgres** (free tier), region `us-east-2`. Connection string in Vercel env `DATABASE_URL`.
- **File storage:** **Cloudflare R2** bucket `vigia-public` for audit PDFs, brand provenance, NFC chip exports.
- **Analytics:** Plausible (cloud), hostname-guarded. Cloudflare Web Analytics as zero-config fallback.
- **Forms:** Vigía first, Formspree fallback.
- **Search:** JSON-LD feeds Google + Perplexity + ChatGPT + AI Overviews.
- **Sign-up reference:** every account, env var, and DNS record needed lives in `SIGNUP-GUIDE.md` at repo root. Read that before touching DNS.

### 07.3 Repository layout (at root of `c:\Users\Stu\raineylaguna`)
```

404.html              bilingual 404
scripts/              build + validation + OG rendering
```

### 07.4 Build + validation
- `node scripts/build-en.mjs` — generate English mirror.
- `node scripts/check-html.mjs` — validate JSON-LD + inline JS.
- `node scripts/check-en.mjs` — validate English mirror.
- `node scripts/check-contrast.mjs` — WCAG AA audit for the nine moods.
- `node scripts/render-og.mjs` — Playwright OG image renderer.

### 07.5 Deploy preconditions
- `favicon.ico` + `og-image.png` present.
- `scripts/check-html.mjs` + `check-en.mjs` + `check-contrast.mjs` + `check-jsonld.mjs` all exit `0`.

- Plausible domain set in `<meta name="plausible-domain">`.

## 08 · Operational rules

### 08.1 Response SLAs
- WhatsApp: **same-day**, always, even if the answer is "working on it, answer tomorrow."
- Email: within 24 hours, weekdays.
- Urgent client-site-down: within 2 hours, any day.

### 08.2 Contracts
- Minimum Care term: 6 months.
- Cancellation: 30 days' notice.
- Every client with a site signs the **Commons Clause** (see `COMMONS.md` in the Studios repo). Opt-in. Strongly encouraged, not required.
- Law 29733 (Peruvian data protection) applies to every ingestion pipeline.

### 08.3 Data handling
- PII is never committed to git. Stripped by `scripts/strip-secrets.sh` pre-commit.
- Client email/WA archives ingested into Socio live in an encrypted Supabase bucket with per-client isolation.
- Vigía's competitor-scrape cache is licensed, not redistributed.

### 08.4 Pricing negotiation
- Always quote the anchor price first.
- One counter-round allowed.
- Never drop below 70 % of the anchor. If we need to go lower, we de-scope instead.

### 08.5 Non-competes
- We do not take two hospitality clients within 500 m of each other.
- We do not take two bilingual-schools clients in the same district.
- Clients get first refusal on their neighbourhood annually.
