00 / Overview

The vision.

"Not a clone of any one tool. A new shape entirely."

The premise is simple. Every tool in the modern creative business stack — Adobe's CC suite, Canva, Figma, Webflow, Premiere, Notion, HubSpot, Calendly, Mailchimp, DocuSign — was designed as a standalone product, retrofitted with AI only in the last two years. The seams show. Workflows fragment across fifteen subscriptions. The AI features feel bolted on because they are.

This suite inverts the model. The LLM is the primary interface. The canvas, the timeline, the inbox, the CRM, the form builder — they're all surfaces the AI can manipulate, instruct, and reason over. A single natural-language command bar drives every operation. "Trim the silence, add captions, generate three social cuts, schedule them for next week, and draft the client update" is one prompt, not fifteen tabs.

Built solely for one person — me, my work, my brand voice, my clients. Optimised for nothing else. The first version that's good enough to use daily is the only version that matters; everything after is iteration on a working tool.

Note on scope. What follows is the full ambition. Realistic build time at 30–35 hours/week is 18–36 months for the entire surface area. The phased roadmap (§04) sequences the work so each phase produces a usable tool before the next begins. The goal is never to ship everything; it's to own the infrastructure I use every day.

03 / Roadmap

Build phases.

Six phases. Each one ends with a tool I can use every day. The order is chosen so each phase pays for itself before the next begins — Phase 1 cancels Adobe, Canva, Webflow, and Loom; Phase 3 cancels the business-ops stack; Phase 4 cancels the marketing stack. Nothing waits until "the whole thing is done."

01Phase One
Daily creative work.

Foundation + LLM command bar + image editor + brand kit + templates + AI Magic features + a basic website builder. By the end of this phase, Adobe and Canva are cancelled.

02Phase Two
Video and motion.

Full video editor, multi-track timeline, FFmpeg integration, colour grading, audio mixing, motion graphics, AI captions and auto-cut. Premiere and DaVinci become optional.

03Phase Three
Business operations.

CRM, scheduling, invoicing, proposals, e-signatures, project management. These individually small modules share infrastructure (database, email, documents) and ship together in one push. The whole business-ops subscription stack falls.

04Phase Four
Marketing and growth.

Email marketing, social management, form builder, analytics aggregator, SEO tools, heatmaps, knowledge engine. The full top-of-funnel marketing stack plus the personal search layer, integrated with the CRM and content tools from previous phases.

05Phase Five
Advanced creative.

UI/UX prototyping, vector design, print layout, RAW photo workflow, 3D mockups, dedicated audio production. Specialised tools that aren't daily-use but kill the long-tail subscriptions.

06Phase Six
Platform and distribution.

Plugin system, real-time collaboration, packaging, code-signing, auto-update, analytics, onboarding flow. The transition from "tool I use" to "product I could sell."
