A WhatsApp assistant trained on your menu, your hours, your tone. Replies at 11pm, handles reservations, only escalates what needs your judgement. It doesn't replace anyone — it replaces the exhaustion of answering your phone between covers.

Espejo — the quarterly mirror.

Once a quarter, Stuart eats at your restaurant as a stranger. Photographs the experience, times the service, listens to the room. A four-page report follows — what worked, what jarred, what to fix. Vigía watches your competitors; Espejo watches you.
